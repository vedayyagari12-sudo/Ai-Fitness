Delete Your Physiqo AI Account

Physiqo AI  ·  Account & data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app under Profile → Delete Account. That removes everything immediately and needs no further action.

If you cannot access the app — for example you have lost your device, uninstalled it, or cannot log in — you can request deletion here instead.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email us from the email address associated with your Physiqo AI account. We use this to verify the request is yours. Send to physiqoapp@gmail.com
  2. Use this exact subject line: Subject Account Deletion Request
  3. That is all we need — no other information is required.
Open email app

Please send the request from the address on your account. If you email us from a different address we cannot confirm the account is yours, and we will have to ask you to resend.

What happens next

We will verify your identity via the email on file and permanently delete all your data including workouts, calorie logs, physique scans, and bodyweight logs within 30 days of your request.

Deletion is permanent. Your data cannot be recovered afterwards, and there is no way to restore an account once it has been removed.

What gets deleted

  • Account information — your login credentials and account record.
  • Email address — removed along with the account.
  • Profile data — age, height, weight, fitness goal, equipment, fitness level and training split.
  • Workout history — every logged session, including exercises, sets, reps and weights.
  • Calorie logs — all logged meals and their calorie and macro data.
  • Physique scan results — body fat estimates, overall scores and per-muscle scores.
  • Bodyweight logs — your full weight history.

A note about photos

Photos you submit for physique scanning or calorie scanning are never stored on our servers. They are sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) for analysis and discarded immediately afterwards. Only the numeric results — such as a body fat estimate or a calorie count — are saved to your account.

This means there are no photos of you to delete. Everything derived from them is removed with the rest of your data.

Data we keep

We do not retain any of your personal data after deletion. We keep no backups of individual accounts, and we do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or crash logs tied to your identity.

Questions

If you have any question about deletion or your data, contact us at physiqoapp@gmail.com.
