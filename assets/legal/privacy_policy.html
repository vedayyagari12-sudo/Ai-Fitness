Privacy Policy

FitAI  ·  Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FitAI ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What We Collect

  • Email address — collected at sign-up for authentication via Supabase.
  • Fitness data — workouts (exercise name, sets, reps, weight), body weight logs, and calorie/macro logs you enter manually.
  • Photos — images you submit for Calorie Scanning or Physique Scanning. These are sent to our AI service (Google Gemini) for analysis and are not permanently stored on our servers. Raw images are discarded after analysis; only the numeric results are saved.
  • Profile data — age, height, weight, fitness goal, equipment, and fitness level entered during onboarding.
  • Usage data — we do not collect analytics, crash logs, or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide personalised workouts, calorie tracking, and physique analysis.
  • To calculate and display your progress on the dashboard.
  • To power the AI streak, workout generator, and recommendation features.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS) with row-level security — you can only access your own records.
  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. The app enforces HTTPS-only connections.
  • Workout data is additionally stored in a PostgreSQL database on our backend server hosted on Render.com.
  • We do not store payment card data (the app is currently free).

4. Photos & AI Processing

When you use the Calorie Scanner or Physique Scanner, your photo is transmitted securely to Google's Gemini AI API for analysis. Google's data processing is governed by their own privacy policy. The photo is used solely for generating the analysis response and is not retained by Google beyond the API call.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

  • You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account within the app.
  • Deletion removes: your workout history, calorie logs, physique scan results, body weight logs, and profile data.
  • To request manual deletion or export of your data, email us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

FitAI is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Google Gemini API — AI image analysis for food and physique scanning.
  • Render.com — backend server hosting.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
